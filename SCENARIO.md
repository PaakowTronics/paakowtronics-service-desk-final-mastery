# PaakowTronics Service Desk — Scenario

PaakowTronics has an internal Service Desk responsible for recording and routing technology support work.

The Service Desk is moving its operational documentation into Git so that changes can be reviewed, traced and recovered when mistakes happen.

The learner is acting as a support team member who has been asked to update a priority policy while several related documentation changes are being developed by other team members.

The repository deliberately contains enough history to require investigation rather than command memorisation.

The final assessment tests whether the learner can:

- orient themselves in an unfamiliar repository;
- read project documentation;
- create isolated work;
- inspect history;
- integrate another person's work;
- resolve a realistic conflict;
- recover a seemingly lost commit;
- understand a remote;
- verify the final state;
- explain their decisions.
